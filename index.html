Roller blinds

A single sheet of fabric that rolls onto a tube, worked by a bead chain. Choose sheer, dim-out, linen-textured or full blackout. The most popular choice for bedrooms, studies and offices.

from ₹120 / sq ft

Zebra blinds

Alternating sheer and solid bands. Line them up to let light in, offset them for privacy, without lifting the blind. Suits living rooms and bedrooms.

Wooden & venetian blinds

Horizontal slats that tilt to steer light. Real wood, wood-grain finishes and 25 mm aluminium for kitchens and bathrooms.

aluminium from ₹950 / piece · wood-grain from ₹1,200

Bamboo, jute & chick blinds

Natural bamboo, jute and PVC chick blinds for balconies, verandahs and rooms that want a warm, earthy look.

bamboo chick from ₹65 / sq ft

Blackout blinds

Vertical blinds

How it works.

  1. Send a photo. WhatsApp a picture of your window to Aashik, or call.
  2. We measure. We visit, measure every window and help you pick the fabric and type.
  3. You get a quote. Priced per square foot or per piece, depending on the blind.
  4. We make and fit. Your blinds are made to size and installed by our own team.
How much do blinds cost in Mumbai?
It depends on the type and size. Bamboo chick blinds start at ₹65 per sq ft, roller blinds at ₹120 per sq ft and monsoon blinds at ₹185 per sq ft. Aluminium venetian blinds start at ₹950 per piece. We quote exactly after measuring.
Do you repair old blinds?
Yes. We repair venetian, roller and vertical blinds, including ones we didn't install, and replace broken parts.
Can you print my own photo on a blind?
Yes. Send any image: a deity for the mandir, artwork, a family photo or a pattern. We print it on a roller blind sized to your window.
Which blinds are best for the monsoon?
Transparent PVC monsoon blinds keep rain off balconies and terraces. Bamboo and PVC chick blinds handle humidity well indoors.
Which blinds are best for bedrooms?
Blackout roller blinds block the most light. Zebra blinds are a good middle ground if you want daylight with privacy.
What is the difference between roller and zebra blinds?
A roller blind is one sheet of fabric that rolls up and down. A zebra blind has alternating sheer and solid bands, so you can let light in or block it without lifting the blind.
Which blinds are best for kitchens and bathrooms?
25 mm aluminium venetian blinds, from ₹950 per piece. They handle steam and moisture and wipe clean easily.
Which areas do you cover?
Our shop is in Ashok Nagar, Kandivali East. We fit blinds across all of Mumbai, plus Thane, Navi Mumbai and Mira-Bhayandar.
